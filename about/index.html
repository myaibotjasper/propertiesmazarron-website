Why Costa Cálida?

The Costa Cálida is situated in the South East of Spain and is home to numerous tranquil, unspoilt resorts. The area is easily accessible from either Alicante airport (75 minutes) or Corvera airport (50 minutes).

Mazarrón, Puerto de Mazarrón, La Azohía, Isla Plana and Bolnuevo are just a few great places to buy properties. Offering stunning beaches, boating facilities, excellent restaurants and the historical cities of Murcia and Cartagena just a short drive away, the Costa Cálida is the perfect place to buy your Spanish home.
